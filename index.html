WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Sustainable Quality & Community

We believe exceptional coffee starts with ethical relationships at the farm level and ends with deliberate craftsmanship in your cup.

A Sanctuary for Coffee Enthusiasts

Founded in 2021 by a team of competitive baristas and agricultural enthusiasts, AromaCraft was built on a simple vision: to make world-class specialty coffee approachable, transparent, and sustainably sourced.

We travel directly to high-altitude estates in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Costa Rica, paying premiums well above fair-trade minimums. Back in our local roastery, every lot is roasted in small 5kg batches, honoring the soil and microclimate where the beans matured.

Direct Trade Sourcing
